Compare daily site traffic and look for peaks in concurrent players. The KraftInk admin panel brings those figures together in one dashboard.

- Review daily visitors, page views and referral domains.
- Filter by date or server and compare with the previous period.
- Export daily totals as CSV. Where your server connection reports them, the KraftInk scheduler can collect player counts and performance metrics.

Open **Extensions → Statistics** and choose the period you want to review.
